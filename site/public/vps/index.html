    How do I pay?
    You send USDC on Base or Solana from any wallet or exchange. The page shows the exact amount, the network and the address, with a QR code your wallet can scan. We check the blockchain ourselves, so there's no checkout site, no account and no invoice email. The price covers the first month.
    
      
    Who holds my keys?
    You do. The keys are created on your server and never leave it. The AI plans trades but never signs them, and anything over your limits needs a confirm code from you.
    
      
    Can you get into my server?
    Only during install. We log in once with the server's first password to install Fren, then lock that password and turn password logins off. After that, nobody can sign in to the server over the internet, us included; you reach your Fren through your own Telegram bot.
    
      
    What happens after the first month?
    The price covers one month. If the server isn't renewed, it shuts down and Fren stops trading. Your funds stay in your wallets, and you keep the key backup you downloaded.
    
      
    Does it start with real money?
    No. Fren is in beta, so every one starts in paper mode: real prices, pretend money. Limits from day one: $500 a trade, 20x max leverage, and a confirm code for anything over $50. Real trading stays off until you switch it on.
